Sakshi, the witness

A live, ONDC-style shopping network. Buyer and seller apps sign every promise they make to each other, and a witness seals each one into a public log that nobody can quietly rewrite, not even the witness itself. Watch it run, then try to break it.

    How an order becomes evidence

    1. Quote

      The seller app signs the price. A signed quote is binding on the seller.

    2. Contract

      On confirmation the seller signs the terms. The buyer app checks they match the quote, then countersigns.

    3. Sealed

      The witness checks both signatures and appends the contract to the Merkle log, returning a proof.

    4. Delivery

      The seller signs "delivered at", the buyer confirms, and that receipt is sealed too.

    5. Settled

      Broken promises are decided from the sealed evidence, and every verdict is logged as well.
